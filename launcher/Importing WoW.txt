Importing World of Warcraft into Wolkara

Close the game in the source app before moving its files.

In Files, move the whole "World of Warcraft" folder into this Wolkara folder.
Keep Data, _forever_ and the hidden .build.info file together. Do not put the
folder inside a second folder also named "World of Warcraft".

Open Wolkara and select the same edition, region and language as before.
App preferences and saved passwords do not move with the game files.

You can delete this guide after importing the game.
